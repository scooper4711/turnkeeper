# Turnkeeper Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. This policy covers Turnkeeper for iPad and Mac.

**Turnkeeper collects no data.** It has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
third-party code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

## What stays on your device

- **Your campaigns, parties, encounters and notes** are saved on your device, and in the files you export.
- **Your settings** and the last campaign you had open are saved on your device.
- **Voice initiative.** When you hold Hold to Say Initiative, Turnkeeper listens through the microphone and turns
  what you say into a number with Apple's speech recognition, on your device when it can. Apple's privacy policy
  covers speech recognition that Apple performs; Turnkeeper keeps no recording.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## Importing characters from Demiplane

When you give Turnkeeper a character's shared link from Demiplane (or scan the QR code on its sheet on an iPad),
Turnkeeper asks Demiplane for that character's sheet PDF and portrait, the way anyone with the link can. It signs
in to nothing and sends Demiplane no account details; Demiplane learns what any visit to the link tells it, under
Demiplane's own privacy policy. The developer sees none of it.

## Syncing your devices

Turnkeeper can sync your campaigns with your other devices on the same network. The devices find each other with
Bonjour, and you confirm a pairing code. The data goes directly between your devices; it does not pass through a
server.

## Other apps

Turnkeeper can read creature art from the PDFs that Scrollkeeper, a separate app, has downloaded, through a folder
the apps share on your device. Scrollkeeper's own policy covers what it does.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send crash reports for
Turnkeeper to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Turnkeeper collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact
