# Turnkeeper Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. This policy covers Turnkeeper for iPad, iPhone and Mac.

**Turnkeeper collects no data.** It has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
third-party code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

## What is saved on your device

- **Your campaigns, parties, encounters and notes** are saved on your device, and in the files you export.
- **Your settings** and the last campaign you had open are saved on your device.
- **Voice initiative.** While the Voice Initiative screen is open, and only then, Turnkeeper listens through the
  microphone and turns what is said at the table into text with Apple's speech recognition, to pick out players'
  names and initiative totals. It runs on your device when it can; otherwise the audio goes to Apple's speech
  servers for as long as the screen is open. Apple's privacy policy covers speech recognition that Apple performs.
  Turnkeeper keeps no recording and no transcript: it keeps only the initiative numbers.

Deleting the app from an iPad or an iPhone deletes all of this from that device. On a Mac, macOS keeps an app's saved data in the
Containers folder of your Library folder until you delete the folder named Turnkeeper there.

## Importing characters from Demiplane

When you give Turnkeeper a character's shared link from Demiplane (or scan the QR code on its sheet on an iPad),
Turnkeeper asks Demiplane for that character's sheet PDF and portrait, the way anyone with the link can. It signs
in to nothing and sends Demiplane no account details; Demiplane learns what any visit to the link tells it, under
Demiplane's own privacy policy. The developer sees none of it.

## Syncing your devices

When you are signed in to iCloud, Turnkeeper keeps your campaigns, parties, encounters, fights, handouts, notes, custom
creatures and artwork in step across your devices through your own private iCloud database. It is stored under your
Apple Account, subject to Apple's privacy policy, and counts toward your iCloud storage. The developer can't see it.
To stop, turn off iCloud for Turnkeeper in your device's settings. To remove what is stored, delete Turnkeeper's data
under iCloud storage in your Apple Account settings.

## Sharing the player screen

When you choose Share with Players, devices on the same network that enter your code are sent what a player
screen shows: the names in the initiative order, how hurt each enemy looks, handouts you show, and the art of the
creatures in the fight. Stat blocks, hit points and your notes are not sent. It goes directly from your device to
theirs, not through a server. A device that follows a table keeps that art only while it is following, and sends
the sharing device its name.

## Other apps

Turnkeeper can read creature art from the PDFs that Scrollkeeper, a separate app, has downloaded, through a folder
the apps share on your device. Scrollkeeper's own policy covers what it does.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send crash reports for
Turnkeeper to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Turnkeeper collects no data from anyone, including children.
